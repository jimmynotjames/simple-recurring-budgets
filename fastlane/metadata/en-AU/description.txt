Know what you can spend, before you spend it.

Wren makes everyday spending decisions fast and simple. Set your spending limits, then track against them as you go.

Features:
• Easy setup
• Fast expense logging
• Daily, weekly, fortnightly and monthly budgets
• Carry over what you don't spend, so today's savings roll into tomorrow
• One-off budgets for set date ranges (like a holiday or a weekend away)
• Your data stays safe on-device and in iCloud
• iCloud syncing across all your devices
• No internet connection needed unless you're using iCloud syncing

This isn't a business expense tracker, a tax receipt logger or a complicated budgeting system. I don't link your bank accounts or hit you with fancy graphs. I just wanted to make a simple tool that focuses on a few habits, not your whole financial life.

To get the most out of it, pick one or two regular spending habits you'd like to rein in (groceries, coffee, takeaway, clothes, and the like). Then decide the most you want to spend each day, week, fortnight or month. Once those budgets are in the app and you start logging your expenses, you'll be making smarter spending calls in real time.

Download Wren and make every day a little easier.
