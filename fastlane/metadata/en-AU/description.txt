Know what you can spend, before you spend it.

Wren makes everyday spending decisions fast and simple. If you can break your spending goals into daily, weekly, fortnightly, or monthly limits, this app's for you.

Features:
• Easy set-up
• Fast expense logging
• Daily, weekly, fortnightly, and monthly budgets
• Carry over unspent funds, so what you save today rolls into tomorrow
• iCloud sync across your devices
• Your data stays on-device and in iCloud

This isn't a business expense tracker, tax receipt logger, or master budgeting tool. I don't try to link up your bank accounts or throw fancy graphs at you. Your finances are personal, so I won't impose a personal finance system on you.

Download Wren and make every day a little easier.
