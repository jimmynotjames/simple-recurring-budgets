Know what you can spend, before you spend it.

Wren makes everyday spending decisions fast and simple. If you can organise your spending goals into daily, weekly, fortnightly or monthly limits, this app is for you.

Features:
• Easy set-up
• Fast expense logging
• Daily, weekly, fortnightly and monthly budgets
• Carry over unspent funds, so what you save today rolls into tomorrow
• iCloud sync across devices
• Data is kept on-device and in iCloud

This isn't a business expense tracker, tax receipt logger or master budgeting tool. I don't try to link your bank accounts or hand you fancy graphs. Your finances are personal, so I don't impose a personal finance system on you.

Download Wren and make every day feel a little lighter.
