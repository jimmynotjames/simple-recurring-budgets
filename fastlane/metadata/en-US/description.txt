Know what you can spend, before you spend it.

Wren makes everyday spending decisions fast and simple. If you can break down your spending goals into daily, weekly, biweekly, or monthly limits, this app is for you.

Features:
• Easy set-up
• Fast expense logging
• Daily, weekly, biweekly, and monthly budgets
• Carry over unspent funds, so what you save today rolls into tomorrow
• iCloud sync across devices
• Data is kept on-device and in iCloud

This is not a business expense tracker, tax receipt logger, or master budgeting tool. I don't try to link your bank accounts or give you fancy graphs. Your finances are personal, so I don't try to impose a personal finance system on you.

Download Wren and make every day more easeful.
